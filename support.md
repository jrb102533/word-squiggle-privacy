# Word Squiggle — Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email **[wordsquiggle@gmail.com](mailto:wordsquiggle@gmail.com)**. You can also use **Settings → Send feedback** in the game, which fills in your device details for you.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Word Squiggle has no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your device.

**Will I lose my progress if I change phones?**
Progress lives on the device, so it doesn't move to a new phone. On iPhone, an iCloud or computer backup of the device includes it.

**How do I join or leave the leaderboard?**
Join from the **Ranks** screen by picking a nickname. To leave, go to **Settings → Leaderboard → Leave**: your nickname is removed and your past scores show as an anonymous "Player".

**I saw an offensive nickname on the leaderboard.**
Email us the nickname and which board it was on, and we'll remove it.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the app removes your progress. For the leaderboard, see the question above, or email us your nickname before leaving and we'll delete your scores too. See the [privacy policy](./) for details.

**Does the game cost anything?**
No. Word Squiggle is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
